# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners of med spas, clinics, gyms, studios and coaching businesses, judging whether a consultant can stop bookings being lost. Two arrival routes: Upwork and Fiverr buyers evaluating Benson (home and case studies), and med spa owners arriving from cold email (`/med-spas`).

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Benson Gitau, an automation and AI systems consultant. It exists to get the right owner to book a call. Success is a booked call from a visitor who understands what would be built for them.

## Positioning

Benson starts from process rather than tools. Five years in operations at Equity Bank taught him that most problems are process problems first: he maps how leads, bookings and payments really move, finds where they leak, builds the simplest system that closes the gap, and hands everything over in the client's own accounts, documented.

The first thing a visitor should believe: this person sees exactly where my bookings get lost (confirmed by Benson).

## Operating Context

Owners read this between treatments, classes and client calls, often on a phone. Upwork buyers compare it against other freelancers' profiles and portfolios. Cold-email visitors arrive with one question and little patience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services and all copy are fixed in `docs/build-spec.md`, which is the source of truth. Clients are anonymous unless approved. Tools covered include GoHighLevel, n8n, Zapier, Retell AI and Mindbody.

## Brand Commitments

Colours and fonts are fixed by the spec's Design tokens. The site must never read as a generic AI-agency SaaS site (confirmed by Benson): no hype, no robot imagery, no gradient-startup energy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portrait: `src/assets/photos/benson.png`.
- Client results: ~80% less manual oversight, ~40% lower operational overhead, a 15% shipping-cost gap found, 10+ hours saved per week. All e-commerce and operations clients.
- Three wellness builds are demos and must always be labelled "Demo build". They have no achieved results.
- No testimonials, prices, client names or wellness-client results exist. None may be invented.
- The intro video and demo videos are not supplied yet.

## Product Principles

1. Show the leak before the fix.
2. Honesty is the sales tool: demo work is labelled, and missing proof stays missing.
3. Owner's language first; tool names are supporting evidence.
4. One action throughout: book a call.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA in both themes, full keyboard use, reduced motion and reduced transparency respected, usable from 320px.
